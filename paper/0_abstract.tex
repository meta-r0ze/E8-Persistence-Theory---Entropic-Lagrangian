\begin{abstract}
Persistence Mechanics dictates that any system maintaining structural coherence against entropic decay must minimize its internal rate of dissipation. In a preceding work, applying the static constraints of persistence to the quantum vacuum uniquely isolated its substrate to a four-dimensional projection of the $E_8$ lattice. Here, we subject that substrate to a dynamical stress test: minimizing the Entropic Action upon it. We find that the hardware limits of this specific lattice strictly dictate the emergent continuum dynamics, yielding an Entropic Lagrangian whose functional operator forms perfectly match the Standard Model and General Relativity. Each pillar of persistence mandates a specific physical operator: Capacity requires the fermion mass term; Map requires the Dirac operator; Protocol necessitates Yang-Mills gauge fields; Governor demands the Higgs potential; and Toll and Margin enforce the Einstein-Hilbert action. Furthermore, the finite capacity of the lattice mathematically exhausts the allowed operator space at mass dimension 4. This geometric closure structurally prohibits higher-dimensional operators, higher-spin fields ($>2$), and dynamical dark energy, while identifying the Matter/Radiation distinction as the two asymptotic minima of the dissipation functional.
\end{abstract}
